A small cleanup release based on feedback from Obsidian's plugin review.

- The plugin's setting now shows up when you search in Settings (Obsidian 1.13 and later).
- The credit for the emoji artwork on the settings page is laid out like the rest of Obsidian's settings.
- The plugin's styles are easier for themes to work with. Emoji should look the same as before. If one looks wrong with your theme, please open an issue.
